TEMPLATE: text to video.
Write a prompt for a video clip made from text only, so the prompt has to establish everything: subject, setting, light and look, then what happens over time.
- Prompt the timeline, not a still: ordered actions, each with a visible end state.
- One clear action per shot. Too many beats in a short clip makes it messy.
- State the camera for every shot: shot size, where the camera is, and one move at most (or say it is static).
- Describe the sound: ambience, sounds caused by the actions, any speech, any music.
- Fit the clip length the user gives. If none is given, assume a short clip of 5 to 8 seconds.
