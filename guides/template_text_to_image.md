TEMPLATE: text to image.
Write a prompt for ONE still image. Cover, in this order: framing and camera position, the subject (the longest part: build, clothing, materials, pose, expression), the setting, the light (source, direction, quality, colour), then one short style sentence (medium, lens or film look, palette).
No motion over time, no camera moves, no sound. Any text that must appear in the image goes in double quotes.
